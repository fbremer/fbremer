# Forest Bremer

Research computing and machine learning in Hilo, Hawaiʻi.

- 2015 to 2020: research computing at the USDA-ARS Pacific Basin Agricultural Research Center (PBARC). Ran the center's Linux cluster, built genomics software, and trained image classifiers.
- 2026: LLM-assisted software development, and local models on resource-constrained hardware.

**Selected work**
- [transferLearning](https://github.com/fbremer/transferLearning): butterfly wing classification with pretrained CNNs (PBARC, 2019). By this method, Grad-CAM was not helpful for finding human-readable wing features.
- [mvMapper](https://github.com/popphylotools/mvMapper): interactive maps of multivariate analyses with geographic data. Co-designed mvMapper and wrote its web app; co-authored the paper (Mol. Ecol. Resour., 2017).
- [digs](https://digs-hawaii.pages.dev): Hawaiʻi rentals you can get home to by bus. Listings are pulled twice a day and checked against TheBus and Hele-On timetables, from a stop and a time of day you pick.
- [local-llms](https://github.com/fbremer/local-llms): measurements on a 16 GB M1. A knowledge edit in Gemma 4 E2B, a reproduction of Chris Hay's decoupled-attention demo, and a blind comparison of nine models judged by models, with the noise floor measured.
- [Sky and sea of Hilo](https://fbremer.github.io): interactive explainers on Hilo's sunrise drift, the analemma, Hilo Bay's tides and tidal braking, checked against NOAA and USNO.
