# Forest Bremer

Research computing and machine learning in Hilo, Hawaiʻi.

- 2015 to 2020: research computing at the USDA-ARS Pacific Basin Agricultural Research Center (PBARC). Ran the center's Linux cluster, built genomics software, and trained image classifiers.
- 2026: LLM-assisted software development, and local models on resource-constrained hardware.

**Selected work**
- [local-llms](https://github.com/fbremer/local-llms): local-model experiments on a 16 GB M1. A knowledge edit in Gemma 4 E2B, a reproduction of Chris Hay's decoupled-attention demo, and a blind model comparison with a measured noise floor.
- [transferLearning](https://github.com/fbremer/transferLearning): butterfly wing classification with pretrained CNNs and Grad-CAM (PBARC, 2019).
- [digs](https://digs-hawaii.pages.dev): a car-free rental map for Hawaiʻi. Current listings, checked twice a day, against TheBus and Hele-On timetables: which rentals a direct bus reaches from a stop you pick, by time of day.
- [Sky and sea over Hilo](https://fbremer.github.io): interactive explainers on Hilo's sunrise drift, the analemma, Hilo Bay's tides and tidal braking, checked against NOAA and USNO.

**Papers and the code**
- [mvMapper](https://github.com/popphylotools/mvMapper): interactive maps of multivariate analyses with geographic data. Co-designed and co-wrote the software (Mol. Ecol. Resour., 2017).
- [GAG](https://github.com/genomeannotation/GAG): NCBI `.tbl` files for genome submission (GigaScience, 2018). Scott Geib, Brian Hall, and Theodore Derego designed and wrote the program. I fixed reverse-strand translation and phase, added tests, and carried that through the 2.0.1 release.
- [HiMAP](https://github.com/popphylotools/HiMAP): phylogenomics from highly multiplexed amplicons. Second author. Parallelized the MAFFT step and wrote the install scripts (Mol. Ecol. Resour., 2018).
- [Low-cost DNA barcoding of intercepted tephritid larvae](https://doi.org/10.1093/aesa/saz071) (Ann. Entomol. Soc. Am., 2020).
- [ccs-consensuser](https://github.com/popphylotools/ccs-consensuser_v2): haplotype-aware consensus from PacBio amplicon reads. Second author (Mol. Ecol. Resour., 2025). I wrote the first pipeline. Carlos Congrains wrote the published version, which lists me as a legacy contributor.

**Other lab software**
- [soYouWantTheLongestTranscript](https://github.com/popphylotools/soYouWantTheLongestTranscript): keeps the longest transcript per gene from a GFF and a FASTA, for orthology work.
